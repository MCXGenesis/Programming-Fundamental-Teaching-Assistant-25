### PROBLEM:  SPACE FRIEND

Four childhood friends are on a mission to find their lost friend in the bizarre dream world of HEADSPACE. But before they can continue their search, they must face one of the dream world's bosses: SPACE FRIEND. This battle plays out in several rounds. To win this battle, four childhood friends average HP must outlast SPACE FRIEND’s HP. The battle will instantly end if four childhood friends win. Luckily, SPACE FRIEND's HP is always constant. 

Your task is to determine the output of this battle (TOAST, DRAW, VICTORIOUS) every round.
- If four childhood friend’s HP is lower than space friend’s HP , the output of the battle is TOAST.
- If four childhood friend’s HP is the same as  space friend’s HP , the output of the battle is DRAW.
- If four childhood friend’s HP is higher than  space friend’s HP , the output of the battle is VICTORIOUS and the battle instantly ends. Additionally, you must print the output of the battle in this condition.

Input (attack.txt):
- The first line is the space friend’s hp
- The second line contains the number of rounds
- The following line, the first integer holds the HP of SPACE FRIEND, the following integers are the HP of four friends.


Note: You are only allowed to use basic Java features such as loops and conditional statements. Do not use arrays, lists, classes, methods (other than main), or any other advanced Java techniques.
